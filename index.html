What Axder does

Axder listens to incoming customer messages and responds intelligently and instantly — answering questions, quoting real prices from live inventory, booking appointments around a real calendar, verifying payments, and keeping a full CRM/pipeline of every client automatically, without the business owner lifting a finger.

  • Autonomous conversational AI tuned to a specific business's products, services, and tone
  • Real-time inventory-aware sales — accurate stock, pricing, and availability, always
  • Appointment booking & calendar management with natural back-and-forth negotiation
  • Payment verification and order confirmation, gated to protect against false claims
  • Full client CRM — pipeline stages, deal tracking, health scoring, follow-up nudges
  • Multi-platform: WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, and more
